Add these to Vercel Environment Variables:

TAMARA_ENABLED=true
TAMARA_API_URL=https://api.tamara.co
TAMARA_API_TOKEN=YOUR_TAMARA_MERCHANT_KEY
TAMARA_PUBLIC_KEY=YOUR_TAMARA_PUBLIC_KEY
TAMARA_NOTIFICATION_TOKEN=YOUR_TAMARA_NOTIFICATION_TOKEN
TAMARA_INSTALMENTS=4
TAMARA_FEE_PERCENT=6.99
TAMARA_FEE_FIXED=7.5
TAMARA_SANDBOX_MODE=false

Do not commit real keys to GitHub.
